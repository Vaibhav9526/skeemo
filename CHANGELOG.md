# Changelog

## [Unreleased] — Skeemo

- New product: Skeemo, a government-scheme eligibility and benefit navigator, with the chaprasi as mascot and logo.
- New calm UI (no emoji; SVG icons): an Office view, an IsCool-style focus Chat with `@` mentions to call counters, and a Vault.
- The office is now a crisp pixel-art tilemap built by `tools/build-skeemo-world.cjs`, with a live notice board of relevant schemes and scholarships whose usual window is closing.
- File vault with local OCR (Windows built-in engine): every paper is classified and judged valid / needs a look / invalid; shows applications and scholarships filed; papers can be used directly at the Documents counter.
- Counters: Reception, Registration, Document Verification (checks + fit to portal size), Eligibility, Form (launches SuperJargon), Help. 11 languages; ElevenLabs voice and speech-to-text.

## [0.1.0]

- First release of IsCool: the Jargon School floor on its own. Teacher, Topper, Smart Guy and Librarian chats (paste or attach images and PDFs), @teach-me, @quiz, @handwritten, @convert and @compress.
